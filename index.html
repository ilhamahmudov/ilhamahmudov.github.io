Hospital Management for Odoo

Featured

A custom Odoo 19 module covering a clinic's full patient workflow: registration → appointment & check-in → consultation → prescription → invoice.

  • Role-based security: receptionist, doctor, manager and billing roles with access rules, record rules and field-level restrictions (billing never sees clinical data).
  • Clinical workflow: appointment states, a waiting-room kanban, double-booking checks, diagnoses, allergies with alert banners, and printable prescriptions (QWeb report).
  • Invoicing: one-click invoices from completed consultations, integrated with Odoo Accounting and fiscal positions.
  • Quality: 40 automated tests, gap-free sequences with a data migration, and views designed to match core Odoo apps.
